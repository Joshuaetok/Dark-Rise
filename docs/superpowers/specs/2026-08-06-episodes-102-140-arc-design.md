# The Dark Rise — Episodes 102-140 Arc Design

**Arc working title:** "What the Ground Remembers" (not spoken on the page —
titling stays per-episode per house style).

## Scope decision

This 39-episode block brings the Ijeoma captivity arc to full resolution.
It also resolves the presence's three-century-old open question (what
happened to its lost guardian ground) by physically converging that
thread with the Ijeoma rescue: the search party finds evidence at the
lost ground tying the hunters who took it to the same lineage/organization
holding Ijeoma, redirecting the search toward the Concern's headquarters.
No new Section 7 historical-skeleton arc is introduced in this block —
oil-exploitation and crown backdrop stay ambient, not foregrounded, so the
existing thread gets full attention. Episode 140 closes with a new cold
hook for 141+, per house style (every episode/arc ends on escalation, not
resolution).

## Continuity guardrails (checked against current CLAUDE.md, as of Ep101)

- Search party (Osadebe, Emenike, Ifeanyi, Okonjo) is four days west of
  Idoro, found the spiral boundary stone, presence confirmed the lost
  ground lies two days further along the same bearing.
- Ijeoma is alone, unaware of the search party, studying the wall's one
  bad corner.
- The Warden's ciphered question ("does the hunt still require the guest
  kept breathing") is in transit to Mfoniso; she has not received it yet.
- The Factor's loyalty has begun to crack but is not yet active
  disobedience.
- Mfoniso is rebuilding her picture of a transformed Idoro from a
  distance; does not yet know the search party exists.
- Section 5.1 confusable-name rules stay in force throughout: narration
  never says the full "Ijoma Concern" in any episode that also names
  Ijeoma (i.e., essentially never, for this entire arc) — always "the
  Concern" / "the House."
- Mfoniso's Episode 82 hunting-lineage tie to whoever took the presence's
  guardian ground is the hinge that lets the lost-ground discovery and
  the Ijeoma rescue become one thread.

## Act structure (39 episodes)

1. **Convergence (102-107, 6 eps):** search party reaches the lost
   ground; discovery there ties the hunters to the Concern.
2. **Complication (108-114, 7 eps):** Mfoniso travels to the Concern's
   headquarters to answer the Warden in person; Ijeoma's first escape
   attempt; the Factor's first small, deniable act of conscience.
3. **Midpoint escalation (115-122, 8 eps):** presence recovers a
   fragment of itself through Chibundu; search party scouts the
   headquarters; a patrol clash blows the party's cover.
4. **Crisis (123-130, 8 eps):** Mfoniso refuses the kill order and
   breaks from the House; multi-front danger to Ijeoma; infiltration
   begins; a real cost is paid.
5. **Climax (131-137, 7 eps):** escape, pursuit, the presence's
   costliest act yet, the journey home begins.
6. **Resolution + new hook (138-140, 3 eps):** homecoming, new status
   quo, Episode 140 milestone closes cold on a fresh threat.

## Episode beats

**102** — Mfoniso's ciphered message is a day from reaching her; she
keeps rebuilding her read on Idoro's defenses while an old unease about
her teacher's silence grows. Ijeoma quietly tests the guard rotation for
the first time without acting. Search party: one more hard day west;
Ifeanyi flags terrain that "isn't empty, just old."

**103** — First physical trace of the vanished settlement underfoot
(worked stone, a buried wall line). Chibundu gets a sharper dream
fragment from the presence. In Idoro, Nkiruka reopens her Episode 94
archive fragment with new urgency now that the party is close to the
same bearing.

**104** — The search party walks into the lost ground itself: a
settlement's ruin, preserved wrong, warped by centuries of grief bound
into the land. Presence's reaction through Chibundu is the episode's
emotional core. Hook: a second spiral mark, fresher than three
centuries old.

**105** — Investigation of the ruin turns up a concealed structure
(cache or rest-camp) rather than a graveyard — the guardian ground's
people were taken, not simply killed. New grief, new question, for the
presence.

**106** — Inside the cache: tools/markings matching what Mfoniso
carries or what the search party has seen of the Concern's methods
(the lineage link made concrete). The search and the rescue become one
mission. Act 1 hook.

**107** — Word reaches Idoro by fast courier. Council debate: pursue
the new lead now, thin as the garrison already is, or hold. Mfoniso
independently decides to deliver her answer to the Warden in person,
mirroring her Episode 92-93 trip.

**108** — Mfoniso's road to the headquarters; flashback-textured
unease about her own training as she senses (without proof) that
something has disturbed the old ground.

**109** — Ijeoma's first real escape attempt: she clears her cell but
not the outer wall. A specific guard lets the moment slide unreported —
first thread of an inside ally. The Factor notices and says nothing.

**110** — Fallout: security tightens; the Warden's lean toward "close
the account" hardens. Ijeoma, undeterred, banks the guard's silence as
useful intelligence.

**111** — Search party's hard travel toward the new bearing; a close
call (bad ground, a fall, a near-miss) that deepens the four men's
trust in each other under real strain.

**112** — Idoro sends formal word to the crown of the new lead.
Nkiruka's dug-up archive fragment sharpens into real dread about what a
founding hunting lineage's fate implies for what's ahead.

**113** — Mfoniso arrives and answers the Warden face to face; learns
more of the four-keepers debt lineage and, without being told directly,
realizes her own teacher's history reaches further back than she knew.

**114** — The Factor's first deniable act: an altered duty roster,
nothing traceable to him. Act 2 hook: the Warden, unmoved by Mfoniso's
report, sets an informal deadline for Ijeoma's fate.

**115** — Proximity to its lost ground pulls a fragment of memory/power
back to the presence through Chibundu — a real gain, paid for in grief
that floods the bond.

**116** — Search party's first sight of the Concern's disguised
headquarters from outside, larger and better guarded than anything they
prepared for.

**117** — Using the guard's earlier silence and the Factor's roster
change, Ijeoma gathers rotation timing and a way to signal outward; the
closest of near-misses yet.

**118** — Udo: Eze Amadi, Nkiruka, and Ejikeme weigh sending help to a
location outside crown jurisdiction; they authorize a small, deniable
support detachment rather than open force.

**119** — The Warden presses Mfoniso for her decision on Ijeoma; she
stalls without yet knowing why she's stalling. First visible crack.

**120** — Search party intercepts a Concern courier/scout, confirming
Ijeoma's presence inside the walled inner courtyard — and that they've
now left a trace behind them.

**121** — The Factor risks more: a near-direct warning to Ijeoma. His
redemption arc's most dangerous beat yet.

**122** — Midpoint hook: a Concern patrol clashes with the search
party's outer watch. Stealth is gone. The Warden sets a hard deadline
for Mfoniso's answer. Act 3 closes on two clocks now running at once.

**123** — Aftermath of the clash: retreat, hide, or push. Emenike's
case for moving now, whatever the cost, carries the room.

**124** — Mfoniso's deadline arrives. She refuses the order outright
rather than delaying — the definitive break from the House.

**125** — The Warden, distrusting Mfoniso's refusal, moves without
her — a second, colder threat converges on Ijeoma.

**126** — Ijeoma's own escape attempt, timed to the chaos of a House on
alert, nearly clears the wall's bad corner and is caught at the very
edge of the compound. Cliffhanger peril.

**127** — Search party's infiltration begins in earnest; splitting up
costs one of them an injury.

**128** — Mfoniso openly intervenes to protect Ijeoma from the Warden's
enforcer, breaking with the House in front of witnesses. Point of no
return.

**129** — Chaos inside the compound: the search party and Mfoniso
collide before they coordinate. The entity/presence reaches further
than Episode 90's precedent, toward ground that was once its own, at
real cost to Oso.

**130** — A real cost is paid — the Factor's decisive act (sacrifice of
safety, standing, or more) buys the opening the group needs. Act 4
closes on Ijeoma and Emenike seeing each other for the first time in
two seasons, still not clear of danger.

**131** — Escape from the compound; the Warden personally leads
pursuit.

**132** — Mfoniso fights her former House outright to cover the
group's retreat.

**133** — The presence's costliest act yet — reclaiming a piece of its
lost ground's power to reach the group at a distance — pays off the
whole three-century thread.

**134** — The group breaks free, but not clean: an injury, a hard
choice, someone nearly left behind.

**135** — The Warden reckons with the loss and decides whether to keep
pursuing; the Factor's fate is settled.

**136** — The road home begins. Ijeoma processes freedom; trust in
Mfoniso remains unresolved. In Idoro, Kene's long-dormant spiral thread
stirs for the first time since Episode 95 — a parallel payoff.

**137** — Final obstacle before Idoro; a last gut-punch or twist
confirms the House isn't finished, or that Mfoniso's status as a marked
traitor now endangers everyone she's with. Act 5 closes.

**138** — Homecoming: Ijeoma and Emenike reunite with the full
household. Emotional payoff, shadowed by what's still unresolved.

**139** — Aftermath: the household decides what Mfoniso's presence
means for them (asylum, probation, distrust — echoing Emenike's Episode
92 reinstatement without repeating it); Osadebe's report reaches the
crown; the entity/presence settle into a changed equilibrium.

**140 [MILESTONE]** — Quiet-but-charged coda establishing the new
status quo; closes cold on a fresh threat seeded for Episode 141+
(the House regrouping, or what the presence's recovered fragment
revealed it should fear next). No resolution on the closing beat, per
house style.

## Build infrastructure change

Episodes 1-101's build scripts each duplicate ~350 lines of identical
OOXML/docx helper code. For 39 more episodes, that's ~13,000 duplicated
lines and 39x the surface area for a copy-paste error. Before drafting
Episode 102, extract the shared helpers (`make_run`, `make_paragraph`,
`build_docx`, `count_words`, `lint_content`, `main`) into
`scripts/dr_build.py`. Existing Episode 1-101 scripts are left untouched
(no regression risk to shipped work). Each new episode script imports
the shared module and defines only `EPISODE_CONTENT`, `EPISODE_NUM`,
`EPISODE_TITLE`, and `OUTPUT_FILE` — the same content-authoring surface
as before, with the boilerplate centralized.

## Per-episode checklist (unchanged from CLAUDE.md Section 10)

Draft → lint (dashes/double-spaces/hyphens) → word count (1,550-2,150)
→ build docx → update Section 5 registry (replace, don't append) →
update Section 11 changelog (trim to ARCHIVE.md past ~20 entries) →
commit individually per Section 12. Never batch multiple episodes into
one commit.
